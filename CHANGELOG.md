# What’s New

## 0.3.4

The first build on the new distribution channel brings together the launcher and the Fabric 26.3 client in one Windows executable.

- **Practice player.** Spawn a local target with a custom name, finite or unlimited health, optional knockback, and a Steve or custom PNG skin.
- **Combat and interface.** Configure aim and trigger targeting separately for players and player NPCs. Choose Large, Medium, or Compact settings menus.
- **World visuals.** Adjust fog strength and Sakura density, switch between local day and night, and customize trails, jump circles, and petal glow.
- **HUD.** Position Astra widgets on a grid, enable compact mode, and show the current Spotify desktop track.
- **Launcher.** Manage Vanilla and Fabric instances, import Fabric modpacks, and install matching Modrinth mods. The single-file download includes Qt and checks this repository for updates.
